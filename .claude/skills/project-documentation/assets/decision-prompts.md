# Decision record prompts

Use these prompts only for a material decision; write actual content rather than leaving a blank template.

- What decision was made, and when?
- Is its status proposed, accepted, superseded, or deprecated?
- Which product constraints and observed facts motivated it?
- What does the chosen approach change for users and maintainers?
- Which alternatives mattered, and why were they rejected?
- What limitations, costs, or follow-up remain?
- Where is the specification or evidence supporting it?
