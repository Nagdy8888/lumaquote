# LumaQuote

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Approved: React/TypeScript/Vite, FastAPI/Pydantic, Python LangGraph, local
Supabase PostgreSQL/Auth/private Storage, OpenAI, LangSmith, Google Workspace.
Docker is limited to Supabase and untrusted file processing.

## Users

Salespeople at a commercial-lighting supplier process customer requests.
Customers clarify requirements through English WhatsApp text, voice, and photos.
Business owners viewing a LinkedIn recording are the secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Turn incomplete customer requests into evidence-backed, accurately priced
quotations with durable human review before sending.

## Operating Context

Local-first implementation and recorded provider-test walkthrough. Google Sheets
owns price/stock data; Drive receives a copy of canonical quotation PDFs.
The initial supplier and all supplied business fixtures are fictional.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Immutable quotation revisions; exact-revision staff approval; staff takeover;
source citations; bounded media processing; recoverable jobs and delivery;
estimated OpenAI allowance $25 total/$5 daily. No payments, invoicing, inventory
reservation, autonomous promotional outreach, or public deployment in v1.

## Brand Commitments

Working name LumaQuote. English interface. Approved graphite and warm-copper
direction, Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Sans, purposeful optional 3D lighting scene.
The three-panel inbox keeps customer conversation, evidence, and quotation close.

## Evidence on Hand

Only local infrastructure and a testing plan existed before implementation.
No measured customer savings, client relationship, or production-readiness claim.

## Product Principles

- Show authoritative business state and evidence.
- Ask about consequential uncertainty.
- Keep humans in control of commercial commitments.
- Make failures visible and recoverable.
- Impress through a coherent real workflow and careful execution.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, meaningful labels, readable contrast, reduced motion,
responsive layouts, and a complete workflow without WebGL.
