# Sales and agent workflows

The initial supplier, products and customer records are explicitly synthetic.
These instructions describe local behavior, not a completed live WhatsApp pilot.

This guide is for operating the sales desk. Developers can follow the detailed
[backend transaction](reference/BACKEND.md), [agent participation](reference/AGENT_RUNTIME.md),
[media lifecycle](reference/MEDIA.md) and [frontend state](reference/FRONTEND.md)
references to understand how these actions remain consistent across refreshes,
staff edits, provider failures and process restarts.

## Manual quotation

1. Sign in using the local staff account generated by `npm run setup`. Open
   **New request** and enter the customer identity and request. The API determines
   company, channel and local recipient; it also queues preparation work.
2. In **Inbox**, select catalog products and quantities. Price and stock come
   from server records. A draft requires one consistent commercial snapshot;
   discontinued products, unavailable quantities and incompatible pack sizes fail.
3. Save a revision. Review its lines, source pages, totals, terms, recipient and
   validity. **View** opens the authorized original catalog; **View PDF** opens
   the canonical quotation. Stock is observed, never reserved.
4. Approve or reject the exact displayed revision. Approval records the staff
   decision and quotation outbox intent atomically. Rejection requires staff
   follow-up and creates no quotation send authority.
5. Run the native worker. Local dispatch becomes **Delivered in local test**.
   Submission and delivery remain separate states for future real adapters.

Defaults are USD, tax excluded, separately listed shipping, seven-day validity,
no default discount, admin-only discounts up to 10%, and staff-confirmed delivery
dates. Arithmetic uses Decimal on the server; the browser does not supply price.

## Notes, human replies and takeover

**Staff note** is internal and cannot impersonate the customer. **Reply** requires
**Take over** and queues a human-authored message. Its author must still have
active company membership at dispatch. **Test reply** simulates a customer only
in the local-test channel; it is not a live messaging endpoint.

Takeover blocks queued automation and automated quotation approval. **Resume
agent** explicitly restores ownership, blocks queued human replies that no longer
have the matching ownership, and queues permitted preparation. It does not grant
approval to an existing quotation.

Customer edits and attachments supersede affected offers and block queued sends.
Dirty editor values survive background refreshes. A 409 conflict leaves the local
draft intact; review the new state, then use **Discard local edits & load latest**
before intentionally editing the newer version.

## Agent requests and evidence

With paid AI disabled, extraction is an explicitly limited exact-SKU baseline.
When enabled, bounded structured OpenAI extraction is still untrusted input.
Missing or uncertain consequential fields produce fixed safe questions rather
than model-authored commercial promises.

An unknown-SKU customer can receive up to five descriptive catalog candidates.
Lookup prefers exact codes, uses explicit watt/CCT/IP constraints, excludes
retired evidence and returns original-page/source-version provenance. Candidates
are suggestions, not confirmed equivalents. The customer must choose a SKU and
quantity in writing; similarity cannot authorize a final line or price.

Lookup is lexical first, with an optional paid semantic fallback when current
vectors exist. The generated 132-query lexical development baseline is not
the frozen 80-scenario evaluation and does not establish real-customer accuracy.

## Voice and product-label photos

The current working-tree attachment control offers **Voice note** and **Label
photo**. Upload is explicit. Audio is limited to 10 MB/120 seconds; images to
5 MB and supported JPEG/PNG/WebP content. Actual bytes are decoded in an offline,
resource-bounded container; names and claimed MIME types are not trusted.

The private original stays linked to its source message. Staff can request
normalized WAV playback or JPEG preview after successful processing. With paid
AI disabled, the UI says so and staff can continue manually. Enabling it allows
one reserved transcription or label-reading attempt. Photo readings preserve
visible text/uncertainty and do not infer identity from product appearance.

Media alone, or a generic **yes**, cannot confirm consequential SKU/quantity
fields. The current gate requires matching written values such as `3 DL-001`.
Human staff can still prepare a manual draft; final delivery needs exact review.

Voice, photo API and synthetic provider canaries have local evidence. The photo
browser path and full media regression have passed with paid AI disabled. Desktop
and portrait Axe checks found no violations on the tested quotation flow; this is
not a complete accessibility certification or photo-reading accuracy benchmark.
